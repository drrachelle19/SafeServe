

## About SafeServe

# 🍽️ SafeServe

### Dine Smart. Dine Safe.

**SafeServe is a smart restaurant ordering and food-allergy awareness platform designed to transform the traditional dining experience through digital technology.**

It connects customers, restaurant staff, and kitchen teams through one integrated system, making restaurant ordering faster, improving communication, providing live food preparation updates, and supporting better food-allergy awareness.

Customers can order directly from a tablet provided at their table or scan a QR code using their own smartphones. No need to wait for a waiter to come, take the order, and manually communicate it to the kitchen.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Traditional restaurant dining often involves challenges that affect both customers and restaurant operations.

- **Long Waiting Times:** Customers may wait for an available waiter before they can even place an order.
- **Manual Order Communication:** Waiters must take orders and communicate them to kitchen staff, creating opportunities for misunderstandings and mistakes.
- **Forgotten or Delayed Orders:** Busy restaurant environments can result in orders being delayed, misplaced, or forgotten.
- **Limited Order Visibility:** Customers often have no clear information about whether their food has been received, is being prepared, or is ready.
- **Food Allergy Risks:** Customers may struggle to communicate allergies, while restaurants may lack organized ingredient and allergen information.

## 🚀 Our Solution

SafeServe digitizes the restaurant ordering journey by connecting the dining table directly to the restaurant's ordering and kitchen management system.

Customers can browse menus, place orders, declare allergies, and monitor food preparation progress without repeatedly asking staff for updates.

Restaurant staff receive organized digital orders, while kitchen teams can access relevant order details and allergy alerts.

---

## 📱 Two Ways to Order

SafeServe provides two convenient ways for customers to access the same digital ordering platform.

### 1. Restaurant-Provided Tablets

Restaurants can place tablets, such as iPads or Android tablets, on dining tables with SafeServe open in the browser or installed as an application.

Customers can:
- Browse the restaurant menu.
- Explore food categories and ingredients.
- Declare food allergies.
- Add meals to their cart.
- Submit orders directly.
- Track food preparation progress.

### 2. QR Code Ordering

Customers who prefer using their own smartphones can scan the unique QR code assigned to their table.

The QR code opens the restaurant's digital menu directly in the customer's browser, without requiring an application installation.

Both ordering methods connect to the same restaurant, table, menu, ordering system, and kitchen dashboard.

## 🛡️ Safety and Responsible Design

Food safety is a central part of SafeServe.

The platform is designed to support better communication and awareness of potential food-allergy risks.

It distinguishes between:

- **Known Allergen Match:** A recorded ingredient is associated with an allergen declared by the customer.
- **Unknown or Unverified Information:** Ingredient information is missing or has not been verified.
- **No Recorded Match:** No matching allergen is identified in the available ingredient records.

**Important:** SafeServe does not guarantee that a meal is allergen-free. Ingredient records may be incomplete, and cross-contact can occur during food preparation. Restaurant staff must review allergy alerts and verify food preparation requirements before serving customers.

## 🔄 How SafeServe Works

The platform connects the entire restaurant dining and food preparation journey.

1. **Access the Menu:** The customer uses a restaurant-provided tablet or scans the table's QR code.
2. **Explore Food:** The customer browses meals, prices, ingredients, and available allergen information.
3. **Declare Allergies:** The customer identifies relevant food allergies.
4. **Place an Order:** The customer selects meals, reviews the cart, and submits the order.
5. **Kitchen Receives the Order:** The order becomes available to restaurant staff and the kitchen.
6. **Allergy Review:** Relevant allergy alerts and ingredient warnings are presented for staff review.
7. **Food Preparation:** Kitchen staff update the order's preparation status.
8. **Live Progress:** The customer follows the order's progress.
9. **Ready to Serve:** The customer can see when the order is ready.

## 🏗️ Project Architecture

SafeServe is designed around three connected experiences:

**Customer Interface**
- Digital menu access.
- Tablet and QR-code ordering.
- Allergy declaration.
- Shopping cart.
- Live order tracking.

**Restaurant Management Interface**
- Restaurant dashboard.
- Table and menu management.
- Incoming orders.
- Order monitoring.

**Kitchen Interface**
- Incoming order management.
- Food preparation progress.
- Allergy alerts.
- Staff review and acknowledgement.

All three experiences share the same application and database.
